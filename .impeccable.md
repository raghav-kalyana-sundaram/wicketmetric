# Cricket Metrics — design context

## Design Context

### Users

Cricket fans, fantasy players, analysts, and curious viewers who want **fast, trustworthy T20 intelligence** (search, rankings, matchups, scorecards). They use the app in short sessions—often on mobile—with the goal of answering “how good is this player?” or “how do these two compare?”

### Brand Personality

**Precise · Grounded · Confident** — reads like a serious sports analytics tool, not a generic dashboard. Warm enough to feel human; restrained enough to feel credible. Data is the hero; decoration supports scanning and hierarchy.

### Aesthetic Direction

**Stadium-night editorial**: dark surfaces with a **cool blue–slate** cast (not flat gray, not neon cyberpunk). Light mode feels like **bright overcast pitch-side**—soft paper neutrals with crisp ink text and a single confident blue accent. Avoid purple–cyan gradients, glassmorphism stacks, and “hero metric” clichés. Motion is **short, ease-out**, and respects reduced motion.

### Design Principles

1. **Read the numbers first** — tabular alignment, clear labels, contrast for stats over decoration.
2. **One accent story** — primary blue carries navigation and actions; semantic colors only for grades and states.
3. **Tint the neutrals** — backgrounds and borders carry a hint of hue so light/dark both feel intentional.
4. **Progressive disclosure** — home explains the fastest paths (search, compare); deep pages stay dense but scannable.
5. **Accessible by default** — visible focus, skip link, keyboard search (⌘/Ctrl+K), sufficient contrast in both themes.
